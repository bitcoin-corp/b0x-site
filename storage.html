Storage · bStoreX

A bucket that
belongs to your key.

Hosting on b0-x sits on bStoreX, the storage layer of the bWalletX family. It runs on our own Hetzner servers and speaks the S3 API, so the tools you already use keep working.

In development

Your own bucket

Each wallet key gets its own bucket. Deploys write into it; the dashboard reads from it. You can also put anything else in it.

S3 / R2 compatible

Use the AWS CLI, rclone or any S3 SDK. Credentials are issued to your wallet key and can be revoked from the dashboard.

Off chain, by design

bStoreX is fast, cheap, normal storage. It's not permanent. Permanence is what publishing is for.

API sketch

Standard S3 calls.

The endpoint and key format below are a sketch and will change before launch.

Draft
# AWS CLI, pointed at bStoreX (sketch)
export AWS_ACCESS_KEY_ID=bsx_xxxxxxxx
export AWS_SECRET_ACCESS_KEY=your-secret-here
aws s3 ls s3://my-sites/ \
  --endpoint-url https://s3.bstorex.com

aws s3 sync ./out s3://my-sites/my-docs/ \
  --endpoint-url https://s3.bstorex.com
// JavaScript, @aws-sdk/client-s3 (sketch)
const s3 = new S3Client({
  region: 'auto',
  endpoint: 'https://s3.bstorex.com',
  credentials: { accessKeyId, secretAccessKey },
});

Paying for it

Lock or subscribe.

Lock BSV for a term

Lock an amount of BSV in your own wallet for, say, 3 or 12 months. The lock pays for a storage allowance. At the end of the term the BSV unlocks back to you, less the storage fee.

The coins never leave your wallet's control; the lock is a time-locked output you own.

Monthly subscription

Or pay a small amount each month straight from your wallet. Stop paying and your bucket is kept read-only for a grace period, then cleared.

Anything you published on chain is unaffected; it lives on Bitcoin, not in the bucket.

Pricing model

Priced by GB stored per month, at or near our cost. Bandwidth for normal sites is included. Final per-GB prices will be published before launch; we won't put a number here until it's real.
