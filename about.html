About

Built by b0ase,
for developers.

b0-x is the developer front door to the bWalletX family: the place to bring a project you already have and put it on Bitcoin.

In development

Where it fits

One family, one wallet.

b0-x is built by b0ase.com, the studio behind the bWalletX family. It doesn't reinvent minting or wallets. It ties them together for one job: deploying websites.

bWalletX

Our BRC-100 wallet. Your sign-in and how you pay. Any BRC-100 wallet works.

bStoreX

The bWalletX store: themes and packs. Not storage.

bDriveX

Private files, encrypted on your device before upload. Invite-only beta, no backups yet. Not web hosting.

bWebX

Mints websites on chain. b0-x publishes through it.

bMintX

Minting for everything else: media, collections, tokens.

OpNS

The on-chain name system b0-x uses to name versions.

b0-x

The developer workflow. Publishing on chain works today. Import, build, off-chain hosting and OpNS names are planned.

Status

Early access, in development.

Deploys aren’t open yet. The sites gallery on the home page and the on-chain gateway are live; the terminal and docs show the design we're building towards, and they'll change as developers try it. We'd rather tell you that plainly than show made-up numbers.

Early access has no form and no mailing list. Connect bWalletX and you'll see your key; when there's something to try, it will be announced on b0ase.com and bwalletx.com.

Connect bWalletX to join early access. Nothing is stored; this just shows you your key.

Vercel and GitHub for developers, on Bitcoin. Early access; in development.
