What publishing costs.

At ~100 sats per kB. One-off cost for a full version.

The maths

Size in kB × 100 sats. A 2.1 MB site is about 2,150 kB, so about 215,000 sats (0.00215 BSV).

Keeping it down

Compress images and drop source maps. b0-x shows the size of every file before you confirm.

All figures are estimates for illustration. You see the exact cost before anything is written, and nothing is charged during early access.
